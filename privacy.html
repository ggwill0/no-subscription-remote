Privacy Policy — No Subscription Remote

Last updated: November 6, 2026

No Subscription Remote ("the app") is made by William Lankenau. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the developer collects no data. Settings and saved TVs can sync through your own private iCloud account; pairing stays on this device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code. The developer has no server and receives no information automatically from the app. Optional support emails are described below.

Your local network

To find and control your TVs, the app asks for permission to use your local network. It uses this only to:

  • look for TVs on your Wi-Fi (using standard discovery such as Bonjour/mDNS and SSDP), and
  • send remote-control commands, and text you type in the app's keyboard, directly to the TV you chose.

These messages stay inside your home network. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

  • The list of TVs you added (name, brand, model, stable identity identifiers, last-known IP address and, for power-on, the TV's MAC address) and your settings are saved on your device in the app's storage.
  • Pairing keys and tokens that a TV gives the app (or the Sony Pre-Shared Key you type) are saved in your device's Keychain, marked "this device only".
  • For Google TV / Android TV pairing, the app creates a private key and certificate that stay in your Keychain.

You can remove a TV, or forget its pairing, in Settings. Removing a TV syncs that removal. Deleting the app removes its local app storage, but does not delete the iCloud copy; iOS may retain Keychain items, so use Forget pairing before deleting the app if you want to remove its pairing keys.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app uses Apple's iCloud Key-value storage to sync settings (appearance, handedness, haptics, app shortcuts, pad/touchpad mode and selected TV) and your saved TV list across your devices. Saved TV records include names, brands, models, stable identity identifiers, record IDs and dates, and MAC addresses if saved for power-on or identity matching. This data lives in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see or access it.

IP addresses do not sync. Pairing keys, tokens, private keys and certificates never sync: they remain in the Keychain marked "this device only". A second device rediscovers TVs on its own Wi-Fi and pairs separately. Connection-address history, installed-app caches, trial/purchase state and diagnostic logs are not part of iCloud sync.

The app works entirely locally if you are signed out of iCloud. Sign out of iCloud to stop syncing on a device, or use iOS Settings › your Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Account Storage to remove the app's iCloud data where available. Signing out does not erase existing cloud data. Remove TVs in the app to sync their removal; copies on devices still using the app can upload local data again after you clear cloud storage.

Reporting a problem

Settings › Report a Problem is entirely up to you. The app builds a text report from what you choose (TV brand and model, the feature that failed, your description) plus your app and iOS version, your iPhone/iPad model and a short log of recent connection events kept only in memory, with no IP addresses, MAC addresses or device/TV names. A report is sent only if you choose to: Send to Support opens your email app with the full report addressed to Ggwill0@me.com, you can read it there, and nothing leaves your device until you tap Send in your email app. The email contains only the report you reviewed, plus whatever your mail app adds (such as your email address, which is needed to reply). If no email app is set up, you can copy the text instead. The app never sends reports automatically, and reports are used only to answer you and fix problems.

Purchases

The free 7-day trial and the one-time unlock are In-App Purchases handled entirely by Apple. The app reads your purchase records from Apple on your device to know whether the trial is active or the remote is unlocked. The developer never receives your name, Apple ID or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
