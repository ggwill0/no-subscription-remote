One price. Forever. No subscription.

No Subscription Remote turns your iPhone or iPad into a TV remote that works over your home Wi-Fi. No weekly fees, no ads, no account and no tracking.

Free for 7 days · then unlock once, forever · $0.99

  • Try everything free for 7 days. Starting the trial is free and nothing renews.
  • Unlock once, forever, for $0.99. One purchase, on every device with your Apple ID.
  • After the trial, all remote buttons (including power and volume) are locked until you unlock. Adding TVs, settings and Restore Purchases always stay free.

Works with

  • Roku OS: Roku TVs from any maker (Hisense Roku TV, TCL Roku TV, onn., Sharp, Philips, Westinghouse), Roku players and sticks
  • Samsung Tizen OS: Samsung smart TVs, 2016 and newer
  • LG webOS: LG smart TVs
  • Sony Bravia (IP control), including Android TV and Google TV models
  • Vizio SmartCast
  • Google TV / Android TV (experimental): Hisense and TCL Google TV / Android TV models, Philips, Sharp, Chromecast with Google TV, NVIDIA Shield
  • Fire TV / Fire OS (experimental): Fire TV sticks, Fire TV Cube, Fire TV Edition TVs (e.g. Insignia, Toshiba)

Hisense: Hisense Roku TVs and Hisense Google TV / Android TV models are supported. Not supported: Hisense VIDAA OS and Apple TV (tvOS).

Power, volume, mute, channels, arrow pad, Back, Home, Play/Pause, number pad, inputs and a keyboard for typing searches (not on Google TV yet). Haptics, dark mode, VoiceOver and an iPad layout.

Private by design

The app talks only to TVs on your own Wi-Fi and collects no data at all.
