Support — No Subscription Remote

Need help or found a bug? In the app, open Settings › Report a Problem and tap Send to Support. The report fills in your TV brand, the feature that failed and a short, anonymous connection log; it opens in your mail app, where you read it and send it yourself. For a general question, use Settings › About › Send to Support, or email Ggwill0@me.com with your TV brand and model, your iPhone or iPad model, and what happened. You'll usually get an answer within a few days.

Works with

  • Roku OS — Roku TVs from any maker, including Hisense Roku TV, TCL Roku TV, onn., Sharp, Philips and Westinghouse, plus Roku streaming players and sticks
  • Samsung Tizen OS — Samsung smart TVs from 2016 and newer
  • LG webOS — LG smart TVs
  • Sony Bravia — Bravia TVs with IP control, including Android TV and Google TV models
  • Vizio SmartCast — Vizio smart TVs
  • Google TV / Android TV *(experimental)* — Hisense and TCL Google TV / Android TV models, Philips, Sharp and other Google TVs, Chromecast with Google TV and NVIDIA Shield
  • Fire TV / Fire OS *(experimental)* — Fire TV sticks, Fire TV Cube and Fire TV Edition TVs (e.g. Insignia, Toshiba)

Hisense? Hisense Roku TVs are supported (add them as Roku) and Hisense Google TV / Android TV models are supported (add them as Google TV). Only Hisense TVs running Hisense VIDAA OS aren't supported.

Not supported: Apple TV (tvOS) and Hisense VIDAA OS.

Pricing, in plain words

  • Free 7-day trial. Tap "Start Free 7-Day Trial". It's free, nothing renews, and you're never charged automatically.
  • $0.99 once. "Unlock once, forever · $0.99" unlocks the remote forever on every device that uses your Apple ID.
  • After the trial, all remote buttons, including power and volume, are locked until you unlock. Adding TVs, settings and Restore Purchases always work.
  • No subscription. Ever.

Restore your purchase

Open Settings › Restore Purchases (or the button on the purchase screen). Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Your trial date is also restored, so reinstalling doesn't restart the trial.

Setting up your TV

Your iPhone or iPad and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. When asked, allow Local Network access (you can turn it back on in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).

Roku

No pairing needed. If commands don't work, on the Roku open Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps and set Network access to Default.

Samsung (2016 and newer)

The first time you connect, choose Allow on the TV. If you chose Deny, open Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager › Device List on the TV and allow "No Subscription Remote". To turn the TV on from the app, turn on Power On with Mobile (Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings).

LG (webOS)

The first time you connect, choose Accept on the TV. To turn the TV on from the app, turn on Turn on via Wi-Fi (Settings › General › Devices › TV Management, or Settings › Connection on newer models) and connect once while the TV is on so the app can learn its network address.

Sony Bravia

On the TV, open Settings › Network & Internet › Home network › IP control: set Authentication to Normal and Pre-Shared Key, choose a Pre-Shared Key (for example 0000), and turn on Remote start. Type the same key in the app when it asks.

Vizio SmartCast

The app shows a pairing screen and the TV shows a 4-digit PIN. Type the PIN in the app.

Keyboard: open a search or text box on the TV first, then type in the app. Letters, numbers and common symbols are supported; accented characters and emoji are skipped.

Google TV / Android TV (experimental)

The TV shows a 6-character code. Type it in the app. Support for these TVs is experimental; if pairing fails, use Settings › your TV › Forget pairing and try again.

Switching inputs: tap Input to open the TV's input list (on TCL, the same list as the TV remote's Input button), then pick the input on the TV. If nothing appears, press and hold Input for other methods: "Home, then Input key", "Standard Input key" and "TV settings". Tell us with Settings › Report a Problem which one worked on your TV.

App shortcuts: Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Paramount+, Hulu, Pluto TV and Tubi open directly. Max and Apple TV open through their web links, so the first time the TV may ask which app to use: pick Max (or Apple TV) and choose Always, and it opens directly from then on. Rumble is hidden on Google TV because it has no link that opens the app. If the TV drops the connection after opening an app, the remote reconnects by itself.

Keyboard: not available on Google TV yet. Typing wasn't reliable on these TVs, so the Keyboard button is greyed out; use the TV's on-screen keyboard with the arrow pad for now.

Fire TV (experimental)

Works with Fire TV sticks, Fire TV Cube and Fire TV Edition TVs on the same Wi-Fi. When you add it, the TV shows a 4-digit PIN; type it in the app. This uses the same local connection as Amazon's phone app, which Amazon doesn't document, so it may stop working after a Fire TV update. Volume buttons work only if your Fire TV controls your TV's or soundbar's volume (Settings › Equipment Control on the Fire TV). App shortcuts

The Apps row opens Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Max, Hulu, Pluto TV, Tubi, Apple TV, Paramount+ or Rumble. The app has to be installed on the TV. On Roku, Samsung, LG and Sony TVs the remote checks which of these apps are installed each time it connects and hides the others; if the TV doesn't answer, all of them stay visible. Install an app on the TV and reconnect to see its button. Rumble isn't shown on Vizio, which has no Rumble app, and on Samsung it appears only if the TV reports it installed. Choose which buttons appear in Settings › App Shortcuts. App IDs differ by region and model, so if a shortcut does nothing, open the app from the TV's home screen instead.

Touchpad or D-pad

Tap the small button at the top-right corner of the arrow pad to switch between the D-pad and a touchpad. On the touchpad, swipe to move one step (keep dragging to keep moving) and tap for OK. The app remembers your choice. VoiceOver always shows the D-pad.

Not supported

  • Apple TV — Apple doesn't let other apps control Apple TV. Use the Apple TV Remote in Control Center.

Common problems

  • "Can't reach the TV" — check that the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi, and that the IP address hasn't changed (Settings › your TV). Guest networks and some mesh or "AP isolation" settings block devices from seeing each other.
  • The TV isn't found automatically — use Add a TV › Add by IP address. You'll find the IP address in your TV's network settings.
  • Something doesn't work on your TV? Open Settings › Report a Problem. Tap Send to Support and the full report opens in your mail app for you to read and send yourself.
  • Buttons show a lock — your free trial has ended. Tap any button to unlock once, forever, for $0.99, or Restore Purchases if you already bought it.
